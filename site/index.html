Rxcheck helps pharmacy staff decide whether a prescription image can proceed, needs confirmation, or should be held for review.

Local-first prototype · Not a certificate of authenticity

GREEN

No configured check raised an issue. This does not prove the prescription is genuine.

AMBER

Confirmation is needed. Keep the prescription on hold while a person reviews it.

RED

A specific integrity check failed. Hold the prescription and follow the review process.

What it checks

Signals a counter team can act on

  • 01
    Seen before

    Compares a slip with records held by that pharmacy.

  • 02
    Prescriber details

    Checks registration details against the pharmacy's local registry.

  • 03
    Requested medicines

    Highlights differences from a previously recorded request.

  • 04
    Review workflow

    Keeps unresolved cases in a hold queue with an audit trail.

⌂

Local by design

Prescription checks stay on the counter PC

The prototype runs offline and stores its records locally. This informational website does not accept, upload, or analyze prescription images.

Research prototype — not for operational use.

Rxcheck cannot establish that a doctor issued a prescription. Its registry and drug-risk list are illustrative and require authoritative verification. Clinical, legal, privacy, and real-world performance reviews are needed before any pharmacy use.
